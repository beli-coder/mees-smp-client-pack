# Mees SMP — Lite & Technical

Welcome to **Mees SMP**, a multiplayer survival Minecraft community at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology! This project provides two versions of the same Fabric-based modpack, so players can choose a simpler experience or a more fully featured toolkit.

**Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 · Fabric Loader 0.19.5**

## Choose your edition

### Mees SMP Lite
**For players who want the modded survival experience with fewer client extras.**

Lite includes the server's shared gameplay content, plus a small selection of graphics, interface, and multiplayer improvements:

- **Farmer's Delight Refabricated** — new cooking equipment, crops, recipes, and meals.
- **Naturalist** — more wildlife and animal interactions.
- **Skniro's Furniture** — furniture and decorations for bases and shared spaces.
- **Traveler's Backpack** — portable, wearable storage for exploring and building.
- **Origins: Legacy** — choose an Origin with special powers and trade-offs. This is a compatible port of Origins for Minecraft 26.3.
- **Sodium, ImmediatelyFast, and Entity Culling** — performance improvements.
- **Iris** — optional shader support (shader packs are not included).
- **Distant Horizons** — adjustable long-distance terrain rendering; you can turn it down or disable it on weaker computers.
- **Xaero's Minimap and World Map** — navigation and waypoints, subject to server rules.
- **Simple Voice Chat** — proximity voice chat.
- **Mouse Tweaks, AppleSkin, Shulker Box Tooltip, and Mod Menu** — basic quality-of-life features.

### Mees SMP Technical
**For builders, redstone players, explorers, and anyone who wants a lot more control and information.**

Technical includes **everything in Lite**, plus:

- **Litematica and MaLiLib** — schematic previews and technical building assistance.
- **MiniHUD** — advanced overlays and building information.
- **Just Enough Items (JEI)** — recipes and item uses.
- **Inventory Profiles Next** — expanded inventory sorting and management.
- **Jade, BetterF3, Held Item Info, and Tooltip Scroll** — more detailed in-game information.
- **Zoomify and Reese's Sodium Options** — additional camera and graphics configuration.
- **Continuity, LambDynamicLights, Visuality, and Not Enough Animations** — extra visual polish.
- **Chat Heads, Better Mount HUD, Advancement Plaques, and other UI enhancements.**

Technical is designed for players who enjoy customizing their game and using advanced building tools. Some optional visual features may be more demanding on your computer.

## Gameplay and server requirements

Both editions share the **same gameplay mods and world**. Technical players do not get extra items or gameplay advantages from an exclusive content mod; the differences are mostly client-side utilities and visuals.

The new content mods require compatible installations on the Minecraft server as well as Java clients. **The 0.4.0 editions should not be used as a replacement for the previous optional packs until the Mees SMP server upgrade has been announced and tested.** Once that modded server configuration goes live, a compatible modpack will be required to join, and vanilla Java/Bedrock crossplay will no longer be supported in the same way.

If you prefer fewer graphics features, start with Lite. If you want Litematica, technical overlays, recipe browsing, and more HUD options, choose Technical.
